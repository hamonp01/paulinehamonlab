# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-globe" %} Collaborative research

We welcome collaborations with researchers interested in macrophage biology, cancer immunology, spatial biology, and translational research.

## Clinical & translational collaborations

We collaborate with clinicians, pathologists, and clinical researchers to study macrophage diversity and function in human tumors. These collaborations include access to fresh and archived tumor samples, clinical cohorts, pathology expertise, and translational studies.

## Experimental collaborations

We develop collaborative projects using mouse models and ex vivo tissue platforms to investigate macrophage function and test therapeutic strategies. Our approaches include functional studies of macrophage populations, tissue slice cultures, and preclinical models of cancer therapy response.

## Spatial & single-cell approaches

We are interested in collaborations combining spatial and single-cell technologies to investigate the organization and functional diversity of the tumor microenvironment. Our research integrates spatial transcriptomics, multiplex imaging, flow cytometry, and single-cell profiling.

## Join Us!

We are always interested in hearing from motivated students, postdoctoral fellows, and researchers interested in macrophage biology, cancer immunology, and innovative technologies.

If you are interested in joining our team, please get in touch with your CV and a brief description of your research interests.
