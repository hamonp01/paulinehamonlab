I am a Research Engineer in the "Macrophages & Cancer" team since September 2026. My work focuses on both murine and human projects, aiming at characterizing immune cell populations in cancer. 

I obtained my Master's degree in 2020, specializing in immunotherapies and human diseases. During my studies and through my experience working in hospitals, I developed a strong interest in applied research. I then spent four years working in leukemia research, further strenghthening my interest in translational research and cancer immunology.
