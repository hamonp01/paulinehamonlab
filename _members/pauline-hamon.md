Assistant Professor (Chaire de Professeur Junior), Principal Investigator

Pronouns: she/her

I joined the Cordeliers Research Center as an Assistant Professor in April 2025 to establish the Macrophages & Cancer research team. Before that, I completed my postdoctoral training in the laboratories of Miriam Merad at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai (New York) and Eric Deutsch at Gustave Roussy (Paris). I obtained my PhD in Immunology in 2017 under the supervision of Alexandre Boissonnas at Pierre et Marie Curie University, Pitié-Salpêtrière hospital (Paris), where I studied the role of myeloid cells during inflammation.

Over the years, I have developed a strong interest in macrophage biology across solid tumors, with a particular focus on liver cancer and immunotherapy. My research combines spatial technologies, single-cell approaches, and experimental models to study macrophage diversity and function within the tumor microenvironment. Beyond research, I teach immunology at Sorbonne University and enjoy mentoring students, fostering scientific discussions, and building new collaborations. I am actively involved in the scientific life of the Cordeliers Research Center through its seminar, communication, and IRP committees, and I am always happy to connect with researchers interested in discussing science or exploring collaborative projects.
