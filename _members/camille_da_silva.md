PhD Student

Pronouns: she/her

I first joined the Macrophages & Cancer team for a six-month Master’s internship and had the pleasure of continuing this project as a PhD student. I trained as a biotechnology engineer, during which I discovered academic research and progressively developed a strong interest in cancer biology and immunology.

My PhD project focuses on the role of macrophages in resistance to immunotherapy in hepatocellular carcinoma. I aim to better understand macrophage diversity and function within the tumor microenvironment and to explore how macrophage-targeting strategies could be combined with current immunotherapies to improve treatment responses. To address these questions, my work combines spatial technologies, flow cytometry, transcriptomic approaches, and experimental models.

I particularly enjoy sharing scientific knowledge, discussing ideas, and presenting my research. I also look forward to meeting researchers from different backgrounds and developing new scientific collaborations.
