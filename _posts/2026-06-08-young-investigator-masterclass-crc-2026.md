The **Young Investigator Masterclass CRC 2026** brought together young investigators and mentors for two days of scientific exchange, mentoring, and career development at the Centre de Recherche des Cordeliers.

The program combined mentoring sessions, career discussions, scientific project challenges, networking activities, and one-to-one exchanges. Participants had the opportunity to discuss their projects, share experiences, and explore the many paths that a research career can take.

A particularly valuable part of the Masterclass was the openness of the discussions. Beyond scientific achievements, mentors shared their perspectives on the challenges, decisions, successes, and failures that have shaped their careers.

A huge thank you to all the mentors and participants for their enthusiasm and engagement, and to everyone who contributed to making these two days possible.

We look forward to seeing the collaborations, projects, and careers that will emerge from the conversations started during the Masterclass.
