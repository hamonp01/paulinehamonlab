Congratulations to **[Camille Da Silva]({{ '/members/camille_da_silva.html' | relative_url }})** on being awarded a PhD fellowship!

After joining the Macrophages & Cancer team as a Master's student, Camille will continue her research with us as our **first PhD student**.

Her PhD project will focus on understanding the role of macrophages in liver cancer, with a particular interest in how macrophage diversity and function influence tumor progression and response to therapy.

This fellowship marks an important milestone for Camille and for our young team. Congratulations again, Camille!
