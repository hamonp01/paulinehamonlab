We are grateful to the **Fondation pour la Recherche Médicale (FRM)** for supporting our research through its **Amorçage de Jeunes Équipes (AJE)** program.

This funding will support the development of our research program investigating the role of **macrophages in liver cancer** and their contribution to response and resistance to immunotherapy.

Our goal is to better understand the diversity and function of macrophages within the tumor microenvironment and ultimately identify new therapeutic strategies that could improve responses to cancer immunotherapy.

The FRM AJE funding will also contribute to the growth of the Macrophages & Cancer team and support the development of our experimental and translational research program.

We are very grateful to the FRM for their support and to all the mentors, collaborators, and colleagues who contributed to the development of this project.
