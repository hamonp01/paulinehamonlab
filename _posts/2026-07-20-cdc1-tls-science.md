We are pleased to share our new study published in **Science**, exploring how dendritic cells regulate tertiary lymphoid structures (TLS) in cancer.

In this work, we show that mature **cDC1 play a central role in the development and maintenance of TLS**. By coordinating antigen presentation to both CD4⁺ and CD8⁺ T cells, these dendritic cells help sustain functional TLS and support effective anti-tumor immune responses.

The study also highlights the therapeutic potential of targeting cDC1 to strengthen TLS function and potentially improve responses to cancer immunotherapy.

This was a highly collaborative effort, and it was a pleasure to contribute to this work.
